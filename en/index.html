This is how
Árkád sounds.

The Music Pilot gives the store a zoned soundscape: a welcome sound at the entrance, curated music in the central zones, nature sounds at the green wall.

Scroll from the entrance to the green wall

The green wall refreshes the air.
Sound tunes the space.

The store’s atmosphere has always been built deliberately: light, materials, plants. The Music Pilot carries the same idea into the audible space — not as background noise, but as zoned sound tailored to the character of each area.

Every zone gets the sound that fits its function. All the guest perceives is that the store feels good to be in — which is exactly the point.

One space,
three sound characters

Six sound zones, each with two or three discreet speakers mounted on the lighting tracks. Every zone is controlled independently — the soundscape changes with wherever the guest happens to be.

At the entrance: a welcome sound

The entrance zone is motion-triggered. When a guest arrives, a gentle welcome sound plays — but it doesn’t announce them to the whole store; it’s subtle, meant for the person arriving.

In the centre: curated music

The central zones play playlists curated by Ulrich Klenke — tuned to Telekom’s sound identity, not a random radio stream.

At the green wall: nature sounds

Around the green wall, birdsong and a soft natural ambience play. The visually calmest spot in the store is now audibly its calmest spot too.

“There isn’t a real bird in there, is there?”

The most common guest reaction at the green wall. The birdsong sits so naturally in the space that guests regularly ask whether something lives among the plants. Nothing does — but the question itself is the best feedback: the sound has become an unnoticed part of the space, and it starts conversations.

The sound starts on its own as you arrive here — the button mutes it. For this demo it’s generated by your browser; in the store, the S12 soundscape plays.

It blends into the ceiling —
it doesn’t intrude on the view.

Matte black speakers on the existing lighting tracks. Installed by S12 while the store stayed open.

Tuned from behind the counter

Staff pick playlists and set volumes from a phone app — zone by zone, independently. If a consultation is under way in a music zone, that zone can be turned down; the birds at the green wall keep singing.

  • Switch playlists with one tap
  • Per-zone volume
  • No extra hardware or console needed

Behind the sound: S12

S12 is Telekom’s sound identity partner — they also created the brand’s new sound logo. For the Árkád pilot they produced the entire soundscape, from the playlists to the natural ambience, with Ulrich Klenke as music curator.

  • Sound & playlists — tailored to Telekom’s sound identity
  • Installation — the full zoned system built in Árkád
  • Sound logo — creators of the new Telekom jingle
